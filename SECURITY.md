# Security policy

MailDots protects people's mail; we take reports seriously and answer every one.

## Reporting a vulnerability

E-mail **security@maildots.com**. Please include what you found, how to reproduce it,
and the impact you expect. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

- We acknowledge within 3 working days and keep you informed until it is fixed.
- We ask for 90 days (less for an actively exploited issue) before public disclosure,
  and we credit you unless you prefer otherwise.
- Good-faith research is welcome: test only with accounts you own, do not read or
  change other people's data, do not degrade the service (no load or spam tests).

## Scope

This repository (the MailDots crypto code) and the live service it is part of. Known limits of the
end-to-end encryption are listed openly in
https://github.com/crearthq/maildots-crypto/blob/main/docs/security/zero-access.md —
a report that one of them can be exploited in a new way is still welcome.
